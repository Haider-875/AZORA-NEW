# Plan 6.5: Advanced Motion & Interactive Experiences

## Objective
Choreograph an art-directed motion design system using existing GSAP and ScrollTrigger capabilities, enhancing interactivity without performance lag or scroll hijacking.

## Tasks
1. Hero Stagger & Mask Reveals:
   - Staggered entrance for hero eyebrow, headline words, paragraph, and CTAs.
   - Smooth 3D tilt tracking on the hero operations stage.
2. Scroll-Triggered Section Entrances:
   - Smooth entrance transitions with `IntersectionObserver` / `ScrollTrigger` for cards, section headers, and comparison tables.
   - Dynamic counting numbers with fluid easing on proof metrics.
3. Micro-Interactions:
   - Magnetic-style pull on primary CTAs (`.btn-modern-primary`).
   - Interactive hover illumination on service cards.
   - Smooth sun/moon icon flip during theme switching.
4. Accessibility & Performance:
   - Enforce `@media (prefers-reduced-motion: reduce)` to disable heavy transforms and transitions for users requesting reduced motion.
   - Ensure 60fps GPU-accelerated rendering on mobile.
5. Verify 56/56 automated tests pass.
